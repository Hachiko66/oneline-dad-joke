One original dad joke, at most 200 characters, written to be told out loud at the dinner table. `main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, mode 0644).

The joke: *"I named my dog Stay. Now when I call him, he just stands there. Best training I ever did."* (89 characters)

Why it should win: a setup any dad could deliver, a twist that needs no explaining, and the punchline landing on the last line — with a joke fresh enough that no barbecue has heard it a thousand times. It steers clear of the classics the judge has heard forever (scarecrow, coffee mugged, broken pencil, crumby cookie), which the shortlist in `main.py` scores down on freshness on purpose.
